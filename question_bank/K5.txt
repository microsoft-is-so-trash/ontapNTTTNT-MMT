- It fails because the image exists

Q: Why should frequently changing instructions be placed near the end of a Dockerfile?
+ A change invalidates the cache for that layer and all layers after it
- Docker reads the file backwards
- Early layers are never cached
- It is required by the syntax
= Tối ưu layer cache.
